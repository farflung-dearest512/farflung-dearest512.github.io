Privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

This policy covers In Plain Papers Studio, the private publishing tool of the In Plain Papers channel, and this website. The tool is used only by the channel's owner, to manage the channel's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram and its own Gmail inbox. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What the tool accesses

  • YouTube (YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API): uploading the channel's videos and their thumbnails, captions and descriptions; posting and reading comments on the channel's videos; reading the channel's statistics.
  • Gmail (Gmail API): reading and drafting mail in the channel's own inbox (inplainpapers@gmail.com), such as platform notices and verification codes.
  • TikTok (Content Posting API, Display API): sending the channel's videos to its TikTok account and reading that account's basic profile and video statistics.
  • Instagram (Instagram API with Instagram Login): publishing the channel's videos to its Instagram account, managing comments on them and reading their insights.

How the data is used and stored

Data from these services is used only to publish the channel's own videos and to show the owner how they are doing. Access tokens are stored on the owner's own computer and server. The data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

In Plain Papers Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is handled under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Viewers and visitors

The tool does not collect data about the channel's viewers beyond what the platforms show any channel owner (comments and aggregate statistics). This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics; it is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account permissions page or the matching settings on TikTok and Instagram. Stored tokens and data are deleted when access is revoked.
